# NOTICE — FMP Corpus Versioning

**Canonical current version of the Fractal Metascience Paradigm:**

> Abdukarimov, A. A. (2026). *Fractal Metascience: Foundations and a Research
> Programme for Recursive, Multi-Scale, Human-AI Symbiotic Knowledge Systems.*
> Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18155672

Documents in this repository are **archival versions** developed during 2024-2025.
They contain terminology, formulations, and case studies revised in the 2026 article:

- **Quantum terminology** (quantum-semantic, quantum superposition, quantum coherence
  applied to cognition or documents): treated as analogy or design vocabulary in the
  canonical version, not as validated physical claims.
- **Illustrative case studies with statistical results** (Parts IV/V of the monograph):
  these are worked examples, not completed empirical studies. The 2026 article replaces
  them with a proposed research programme (section 9).
- **"SIMULATION MODE" headers**: artifacts of an early drafting convention, superseded.
